**Date:** 2025-01-27  
**Prepared by:** k05m0navt  
**Status:** Ready for A/B Testing

## Executive Summary

**Pricing Model:** Unified subscription (single tier for all users: players, judges, clubs)  
**Trial Period:** Free trial for 1-month subscription  
**Billing Cycles:** 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months  
**Strategy:** Test multiple price points to optimize conversion and revenue

---

## 1. Pricing Structure

### 1.1 Unified Subscription Model

**Key Principle:** One subscription tier provides access to ALL premium features for ALL user types (players, judges, clubs).

**Rationale:**

- Simpler pricing reduces decision friction
- All users get full value regardless of primary use case
- Easier to communicate and market
- Reduces support complexity

### 1.2 Pricing Tiers (Test Prices)

**Base Price Point:** To be determined through A/B testing

**Recommended Starting Prices for Testing:**

#### Option A: Conservative ($6/month base)

- **1 Month:** $6/month
- **3 Months:** $15 total ($5/month) - 17% discount
- **6 Months:** $28 total ($4.67/month) - 22% discount
- **12 Months:** $50 total ($4.17/month) - 31% discount
- **Free Trial:** 7 days free trial for 1-month subscription

#### Option B: Moderate ($8/month base)

- **1 Month:** $8/month
- **3 Months:** $20 total ($6.67/month) - 17% discount
- **6 Months:** $38 total ($6.33/month) - 21% discount
- **12 Months:** $68 total ($5.67/month) - 29% discount
- **Free Trial:** 7 days free trial for 1-month subscription

#### Option C: Premium ($10/month base)

- **1 Month:** $10/month
- **3 Months:** $25 total ($8.33/month) - 17% discount
- **6 Months:** $48 total ($8/month) - 20% discount
- **12 Months:** $85 total ($7.08/month) - 29% discount
- **Free Trial:** 7 days free trial for 1-month subscription

### 1.3 Discount Structure

**Discount Philosophy:**

- **3 Months:** 15-20% discount (encourages quarterly commitment)
- **6 Months:** 20-25% discount (mid-term commitment)
- **12 Months:** 25-35% discount (annual commitment, best value)

**Discount Calculation:**

- 3 months: Base price × 2.5 (saves 0.5 months)
- 6 months: Base price × 4.67 (saves 1.33 months)
- 12 months: Base price × 8.33-10 (saves 2-4 months depending on base)

## 2. Free Trial Strategy

### 2.1 Trial Period Details

**Duration:** 7 days free trial (for 1-month subscription only)

**Trial Features:**

- Full access to all premium features
- No credit card required initially (optional: collect at signup for seamless conversion)
- Automatic conversion to paid at end of trial
- Easy cancellation before trial ends

**Rationale:**

- Reduces friction for new users
- Allows users to experience full value
- Higher conversion rates than no trial
- Industry standard practice

### 2.2 Trial Conversion Optimization

**During Trial:**

- Day 1: Welcome email with feature highlights
- Day 3: Usage statistics and value demonstration
- Day 5: Reminder of trial ending + benefits of premium
- Day 7: Final conversion prompt with discount offer

**Conversion Tactics:**

- Show usage statistics ("You've viewed 50 games this week")
- Highlight premium features used
- Offer first-month discount (10-15% off) if converted during trial

## 3. A/B Testing Plan

### 3.1 Test Variables

**Primary Test: Base Price Point**

- Test Option A ($6), Option B ($8), Option C ($10)
- Measure: Conversion rate, revenue per user, LTV

**Secondary Tests:**

- Discount percentages (15% vs 20% vs 25% for 3-month)
- Trial duration (7 days vs 14 days)
- Trial requirements (credit card vs no credit card)

### 3.2 Testing Methodology

**Phase 1: Base Price Test (Weeks 1-4)**

- Split traffic: 33% Option A, 33% Option B, 33% Option C
- Minimum sample: 100 users per variant
- Measure: Conversion rate, revenue, churn

**Phase 2: Discount Optimization (Weeks 5-8)**

- Use winning base price from Phase 1
- Test discount percentages
- Measure: Long-term subscription preference

**Phase 3: Trial Optimization (Weeks 9-12)**

- Test trial duration and requirements
- Measure: Trial-to-paid conversion

### 3.3 Success Metrics

**Primary Metrics:**

- **Conversion Rate:** Free → Paid (target: 4-6%)
- **Revenue Per User (ARPU):** Monthly average
- **Lifetime Value (LTV):** Total revenue per user
- **Churn Rate:** Monthly cancellation rate (target: <5%)

**Secondary Metrics:**

- Trial-to-paid conversion rate (target: 30-40%)
- Subscription length preference (1/3/6/12 month distribution)
- Price sensitivity (conversion at different price points)

## 5. Revenue Projections by Price Point

### 5.1 Scenario: $6/month Base (Option A)

**Assumptions:**

- 1,000 total users
- 5% conversion rate = 50 paid users
- Distribution: 40% monthly, 30% 3-month, 20% 6-month, 10% annual

**Monthly Revenue:**

- Monthly: 20 users × $6 = $120
- 3-month: 15 users × $5 = $75 (prorated)
- 6-month: 10 users × $4.67 = $46.70 (prorated)
- Annual: 5 users × $4.17 = $20.85 (prorated)
- **Total: $262.55/month**

**Annual Revenue:** $3,150.60

### 5.2 Scenario: $8/month Base (Option B)

**Assumptions:**

- 1,000 total users
- 4.5% conversion rate = 45 paid users (slightly lower due to higher price)
- Distribution: 40% monthly, 30% 3-month, 20% 6-month, 10% annual

**Monthly Revenue:**

- Monthly: 18 users × $8 = $144
- 3-month: 14 users × $6.67 = $93.38 (prorated)
- 6-month: 9 users × $6.33 = $56.97 (prorated)
- Annual: 4 users × $5.67 = $22.68 (prorated)
- **Total: $317.03/month**

**Annual Revenue:** $3,804.36

### 5.3 Scenario: $10/month Base (Option C)

**Assumptions:**

- 1,000 total users
- 4% conversion rate = 40 paid users (lower due to higher price)
- Distribution: 40% monthly, 30% 3-month, 20% 6-month, 10% annual

**Monthly Revenue:**

- Monthly: 16 users × $10 = $160
- 3-month: 12 users × $8.33 = $99.96 (prorated)
- 6-month: 8 users × $8 = $64 (prorated)
- Annual: 4 users × $7.08 = $28.32 (prorated)
- **Total: $352.28/month**

**Annual Revenue:** $4,227.36

### 5.4 Recommendation

**Start with Option B ($8/month base):**

- Balanced conversion and revenue
- Room to adjust up or down based on testing
- Competitive with market ($5-15/month range)
- Good value perception

## 9. Risk Mitigation

### 9.1 Pricing Risks

**Risk:** Price too high → Low conversion

- **Mitigation:** Start with moderate price, test lower options
- **Monitoring:** Track conversion rates weekly

**Risk:** Price too low → Low revenue

- **Mitigation:** Test higher price points, optimize features
- **Monitoring:** Track ARPU and LTV

**Risk:** Discounts too aggressive → Revenue loss

- **Mitigation:** Test discount percentages, monitor LTV
- **Monitoring:** Compare revenue by subscription length

### 9.2 Market Risks

**Risk:** Local market price sensitivity

- **Mitigation:** Test localized pricing, regional discounts
- **Monitoring:** Conversion rates by region

**Risk:** Competitive pricing pressure

- **Mitigation:** Focus on unique value (judge analytics)
- **Monitoring:** Competitive analysis quarterly
